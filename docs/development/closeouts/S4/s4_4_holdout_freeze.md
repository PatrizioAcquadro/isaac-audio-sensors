## Frozen scientific contract

The nominal target is 75% fit and 25% holdout over 16 condition-cell weights:
12 fit and 4 holdout cells. Approved leakage groups are indivisible, so the
achieved split is 10 fit and 6 holdout cells, or 62.5% and 37.5%. At the group
level it is 6 fit and 3 holdout groups, or 66.67% and 33.33%.

The deterministic seed is `0`. The assignment was generated by the
**S4.4 constraint-aware adapter**, not by the unadapted S2.5 greedy builder.
The existing S2.5 `build_split_plan` implementation, public behavior, and tests
were not changed, and no claim is made that it reproduces this assignment.

The adapter exhaustively enumerates all 512 subsets of the nine groups. It
rejects empty fit/holdout partitions and subsets that do not contain the full
repeatability group, at least one quality-eligible controlled cell, and at
least one quality-eligible robustness cell. Failed or ineligible cells receive
no usable-coverage credit. Feasible subsets are ranked lexicographically by:

1. absolute holdout-cell deviation from the nominal target of four;
2. underfill penalty, with underfill ranked after equal overfill;
3. holdout group count;
4. sum of selected S2.5 seeded score ranks;
5. ascending tuple of selected score ranks; and
6. lexical tuple of selected group IDs.

There is no post-selection adjustment. No outcome metric enters eligibility,
ranking, seed selection, or assignment. For seed 0, 188 subsets are feasible
and the unique global optimum has ranking key
`[2, 0, 3, 9, [1, 2, 6], [g01, g03, g08]]`.

Before the adapter was authorized, all 362,880 possible score orderings of the
unchanged S2.5 greedy builder were checked. None satisfied the approved
repeatability, controlled, and robustness holdout constraints. This result is
preserved in `freeze/s2_5_constraint_feasibility.json`.

The adapter emitted `ias.dataset_split_plan.v1` only after unchanged
`SplitPlan` construction, serialization round-trip, self-integrity, and
`verify_no_leakage` succeeded. The frozen SplitPlan SHA-256 is
`1569c00cbaec57e5625e0876fd243e17a2a67b287b3edf9865e41bb7ce8c0ce3`.

## Seal and access policy

`holdout_seal.json` is deterministic and contains metadata, repository-relative
paths, byte sizes, roles, and SHA-256 hashes only. It contains no raw media,
analysis content, or derived holdout outcomes. Its current file SHA-256 is
`e0758b9820dac2f8ba0d07e2a17ad14a107abf1a11d408e813b034cfa03c13ec`.
Recording the machine-local integrity event did not change this tracked hash.

Machine-local seal state, future grants, and the append-only access ledger live
under gitignored `dataset/S4.4/access/`. No S4.8 grant exists. The ledger has a
hash-chained seal-initialization event and hash-only integrity-validation
events only; none records `holdout_opened: true`.

Repository tooling permits S4.5 access only to fit attempt IDs and denies
holdout access for fitting, validation-driven tuning, threshold adjustment, or
system-behavior changes. S4.4 integrity validation may read bytes only to
recompute size and SHA-256. It reports integrity status, issue locations, and
counts only, returns no content-derived values, does not count as opening the
holdout, and does not modify the tracked seal.

A future grant can be consumed only for `S4.8_evaluation`. It must be explicit,
machine-local, single-use, self-hashed, and exactly bound to the tracked seal,
SplitPlan, purpose, and a passing hash-bound S4.7 preregistration. Malformed,
altered, inconsistent, incorrectly purposed, prerequisite-mismatched,
seal-mismatched, or reused grants fail closed. A valid future consumption must
append a hash-chained audit event.

This enforcement applies through repository tooling. It cannot prevent or
detect direct reads by the filesystem owner. S4.3 outcomes were historically
analyzed before this freeze, so this is not a claim that the evidence was
historically unseen. The freeze prohibits later authorized tuning use and
preserves a fixed evaluation assignment from this point onward.
